Value Creation Scorecard: what the company is worth and what moves it

Enter LTM revenue, normalized EBITDA and net debt, rate the ten factors buyers price into the multiple today and after your plan, and add the EBITDA each lever brings. The scorecard shows enterprise and equity value now and after the plan, splits the gain into EBITDA growth, multiple expansion and cash released, and tracks equity value month by month. Your data stays in your browser.

What drives the multiple

Rate each factor today and where the plan takes it. Weak = −0.25x, Average = 0, Strong = +0.25x from the base multiple; the total adjustment is capped at ±1.5x. A planning heuristic, not a valuation.

Value creation plan

Recurring annual EBITDA each lever adds once the plan is in place, and one-time cash released from working capital (it reduces net debt).

Equity value bridge

How the gain splits between a bigger EBITDA, a better multiple and cash released.

Monthly tracker

Each month, enter LTM EBITDA and net debt. Value uses today's multiple until you update the ratings, so the line shows what the plan has already delivered.
