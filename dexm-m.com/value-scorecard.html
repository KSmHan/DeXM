Value Creation Scorecard: what the company is worth and what moves it

Enter LTM revenue, EBITDA and net debt, then ten measurable metrics buyers price in (customer concentration, owner dependence, scrap, growth and more) today and after your plan. Each metric moves the multiple, four of them also move EBITDA, and the table shows what every change is worth in equity value. The scorecard shows enterprise and equity value now and after the plan, splits the gain into EBITDA growth, multiple expansion and cash released, and tracks equity value month by month. Your data stays in your browser.

What drives the multiple and EBITDA

Enter each metric today and where the plan takes it. Each metric moves the multiple on a sliding scale from −0.25x at the weak level to +0.25x at the strong level (total capped at ±1.5x); both levels are editable, so set them to your industry and buyer. Four metrics also change EBITDA directly: documented add-backs, scrap, management roles and growth. The last column shows what each change is worth in equity value. A planning heuristic, not a valuation.
