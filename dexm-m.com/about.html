ABOUT DEXM MANAGEMENT

Operating experience, applied where it matters most.

WHO WE ARE

DeXM Management brings 20+ years of international experience across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and the CIS in restructurings, liquidity stabilization, EBITDA improvement, and exit readiness across the metals, construction materials, extrusion, millwork, automotive, and food manufacturing sectors.

We are engaged to address operational underperformance, liquidity pressure, and margin erosion. Our track record includes a 230%+ EBITDA improvement, P&L responsibility of up to $520M, leadership of organizations with up to 1,000 employees, restructuring $60M of debt, and a successful exit generating a 2.19x gross MOIC and a 19% five-year gross IRR.

DeXM Management partners with Boards, Investment Committees, lenders, and management teams to protect enterprise value and improve exit outcomes — working alongside PE sponsors, owners and management teams of manufacturing companies to stabilize cash, rebuild EBITDA, and restore and create enterprise value.
