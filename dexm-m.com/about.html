WHO WE ARE

Operators, accountable for the numbers.

DeXM Management brings 20+ years of international experience across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East and the CIS in restructurings, liquidity stabilization, EBITDA improvement and exit readiness, in metals, construction materials, extrusion, millwork, automotive and food manufacturing.

We are engaged when a business faces operational underperformance, liquidity pressure or margin erosion. Our experience includes raising EBITDA margin from 3% to 11.5% (+8.5 pp), P&L responsibility of up to $520M, leadership of organizations with up to 1,000 employees, restructuring $60M of debt, and an exit at a 2.19x gross MOIC and a 19% gross IRR.

We partner with Boards, Investment Committees, lenders and management teams to protect enterprise value and improve exit outcomes.

HOW WE WORK

Four principles, every engagement.

The same discipline whether we are the interim CEO, the diligence team or the people running the first 100 days.

01

Inside the business

We take operating roles and decision rights, and work on the plant floor, not from a slide deck.

02

Cash first

Liquidity comes before everything else: a real 13-week forecast, daily cash control and working capital released.

03

Numbers that reconcile

Every lever is sized from SKU, labor, scrap and cash data, and tracked against the P&L until it shows up there.

04

Built to hand over

KPIs, S&OP, operating cadence and decision rights stay with the management team after we leave.
